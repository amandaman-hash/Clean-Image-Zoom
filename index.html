Clean Image Zoom

Image search results with no captions, prices or icons. Click any image to see it in full resolution, then scroll to zoom.

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

Image Zoom

Show the bar with Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac)

How to use it

  1. Install. Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Search. Open an image search and wait for results to load.
  3. Click the bookmark once. The captions disappear. Click it again after any reload or new search.
  4. Click an image. It opens large on a dark background. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

What you get

Clean gridOnly images, no text, prices or icons.
Full resolutionLoads the original, not the blurry preview.
Scroll to zoom0.5x to 10x, centered on your cursor.
FastStarts downloading when you pause on an image.

Controls

Dragging doesn't work?

Create a bookmark by hand, then paste this into its URL field. It must start with javascript: and have nothing before or after it.

Good to know

This is best-effort software. It depends on how the search site builds its pages, so it may stop working when that changes. Pausing on images downloads their full-size originals, which uses more data. Some results, such as shopping tiles, only show the preview. Desktop browsers only.

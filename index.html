Clean Image Zoom

A bookmarklet for image search pages. It hides captions, prices and icons under results, and opens images full-resolution on a dark background. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, Esc to close.

Install

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it: Just Images
  3. Open an image search, then click the bookmark once per page load.

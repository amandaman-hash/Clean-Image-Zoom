Image search,
without the clutter.

Remove captions, prices and icons from image results. Click any image to see the full-resolution original, then scroll to zoom.

  1. Install: drag the round button to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Search: open an image search and wait for the results.
  3. Click the bookmark once per page load. Captions vanish.
  4. Click an image to open it. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
